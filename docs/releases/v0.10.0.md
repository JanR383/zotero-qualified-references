## Graph and list windows

- **Live update.** Open graph and list windows follow changes in Zotero: new, edited or deleted references and items appear without reopening. Scope, filters, search, zoom and node positions stay as they are. "Current selection" keeps the selection it was chosen with.
- **Export.** Both windows have an Export menu that saves what they currently show. Graph: image (PNG), CSV with one row per reference, or GraphML for Gephi and Cytoscape. List: Markdown with one section per item, or CSV. Exported items carry a `zotero://select` link back to Zotero. A setting exports the PNG with a transparent background.
- **List details.** An expanded list entry shows its pages, a "↗ PDF" link to the anchored passage, and the comment (one line, the whole text on click).

## Undo (Zotero 10)

- Adding, editing and deleting references in the item pane, and adding them from the PDF reader, can be undone and redone with Edit → Undo / Redo. Zotero 9 has no undo history.

## Sync safety

- Zotero's sync server rejects fields larger than 64 KB, and an item over that limit stops syncing. The plugin now refuses a save that would grow an item's Extra field beyond about 60 000 bytes and shows a message instead. Saves that make the field smaller are always allowed.
- Comments typed in the item pane are limited to 2 000 characters by default; the limit can be set between 100 and 10 000 in the settings. Longer comments saved before stay as they are.

## Compatibility

- Requires **Zotero 9 or 10**. The stored data format is unchanged.

## Highlights

- **Graph: search.** "Search items…" outlines matching items and dims everything outside their direct neighbours. Enter centres the view on the first match, Escape clears the search. While a term is entered, "Include neighbours of neighbours" widens the focus by one more step.
- **Graph: filters.** A collapsible "Filters" group hides references by stance, items by type, and items with fewer than a chosen number of references. When the group is collapsed, its title shows how many filters are active.
- **Graph: reference details.** Hovering an arrow shows its stance, comment and pages. Clicking it opens the passage in the PDF, or selects the citing item if the reference has no PDF anchor.
- **Graph: readable labels and node size.** Labels appear for small graphs, when zoomed in, on hover, and for the most connected, highlighted or matching items. Nodes grow with the number of incoming references.
- **Scope "Current selection in Zotero".** On Zotero 10, graph and list can follow the libraries and collections selected in Zotero's collection tree, including sub-collections.
- **Reference list: sort order and stance balance.** The list sorts alphabetically, by number of references or by year (undated items last). Each row shows its total and one pill per stance present.

## Settings

- New group "Controls in the graph window": search, filters and tag highlighting are shown by default; the node-size switch and the edge-length slider are hidden by default. Hidden controls keep their behaviour: nodes stay sized by incoming references, and the edge length keeps its saved value.
- If tag highlighting is switched off, a previously saved tag selection is ignored.

## Fixes

- The "Highlight tags" panel is no longer cut off by the graph's control box.

## Compatibility

- Requires **Zotero 9 or 10**. The stored data format is unchanged. "Current selection in Zotero" is offered only on Zotero 10.

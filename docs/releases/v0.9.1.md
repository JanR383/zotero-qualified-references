## Graph readability

- **Labels no longer overlap.** A label that would cover one already drawn is left out; the most connected items are labelled first, and the item under the mouse and search hits are always labelled. Zoom in to see the rest.
- **Timeline:** items from nearby years are spread out vertically so their labels stay apart, and the year numbers sit at the bottom edge, where the legend and controls no longer cover them.
- **Tooltips** are opaque, follow light and dark mode, and use larger text with a border and shadow. An item's tooltip leaves out "References" or "Referenced by" when there are none.

## Privacy fix

- When an item is added to a group library, the copy now loses every reference that points outside that group, unless "Copy qualified references when adding an item to a group library" is on. Before, a copy kept its references (including private comments) when their targets had been deleted or lived in another group. Items synced from other group members are not changed.

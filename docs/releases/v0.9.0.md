## Highlights

- **Graph: timeline layout.** A "Network / Timeline" switch places items by publication year: each year has a fixed column, a year grid runs behind the nodes, and items without a year sit in a dashed "n.d." lane on the right. Filters, search, tag highlighting and tooltips work as in the network layout. The switch is off by default; turn it on under Settings → Graph window.
- **Clearer settings page.** Settings are now grouped by where they take effect: item pane and list window, graph window, PDF reader, colours, privacy. A short note explains what hidden graph controls keep.

## Notes

- All saved settings carry over; no setting was renamed.
- An item whose date has no year is now sorted with the undated items in the list window, instead of as year 0.

## Compatibility

- Requires **Zotero 9 or 10**. The stored data format is unchanged.
